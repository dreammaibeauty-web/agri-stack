# AgroStack Farm

> A comprehensive guide to agricultural water management in India, covering micro-irrigation technologies, PMKSY/PDMC subsidies, groundwater depletion challenges, and agronomic best practices for Indian farmers, agri-startups, students, and policymakers.

## Primary page

- [Agricultural Water Management in India](https://agro-stack-farm.vercel.app/): The definitive 2026 guide comparing drip vs sprinkler vs flood irrigation, explaining PMKSY/PDMC subsidy eligibility (55% for small farmers, 45% for others), analyzing groundwater statistics for Punjab and Haryana, and detailing cost-effective water-saving techniques for smallholders.

## Key facts from this guide

- Agriculture consumes over 80% of India's usable freshwater, yet on-farm water use efficiency remains 25–35%.
- Drip irrigation achieves 80–95% water use efficiency vs. 30–50% for flood irrigation.
- PMKSY's "Per Drop More Crop" (PDMC) scheme provides up to 55% subsidy for small/marginal farmers (≤2 ha) and 45% for larger farmers.
- Maximum eligible area under PDMC is 5 hectares per beneficiary, with a 7-year repeat cycle.
- Haryana's groundwater extraction stage is 136.75%, with some districts exceeding 200%.
- Punjab water tables are declining 0.4–1.0 meters per year; 111–115 of 153 assessment units are over-exploited.
- 115+ lakh hectares have been brought under micro-irrigation since 2015-16.
- Effective subsidies with state top-ups can reach 70–90% in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh.
